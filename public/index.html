When you gotta go, know where to go.

    POOP maps public restrooms and water fountains so you can plan a stop mid-run.

    Principal Operating Objective Planner

    POOP shows every public restroom and drinking-water spot that has been mapped in OpenStreetMap, the free map of the world that anyone can edit.

    • Restroom
    • Drinking water

    Something missing or wrong?

    Add or fix it on OpenStreetMap and it will show up here within a week or so. On a phone, the free apps StreetComplete (Android) and Every Door (iOS and Android) make it easy.

    Your location

    Your location stays on your device. It is only used to centre the map and sort the list.

    Hours, fees and seasonal closures come from volunteers and can be out of date. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
